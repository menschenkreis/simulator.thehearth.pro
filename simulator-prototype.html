Open Chords: C Major

RhythmLevel 1Chords & Harmony

The C major chord is one of the first open chords every guitarist learns. Three fingers, bright full sound, foundational to thousands of songs.

Ring finger on 3rd fret A string. Middle on 2nd fret D string. Index on 1st fret B string. Strum from A down.

WHERE THIS FITS: C major is the gateway to the key of C — the only major key with no sharps or flats. Connects to G, Am, F. Together these four open thousands of songs.
