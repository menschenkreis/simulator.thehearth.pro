Open Chords: C Major

RhythmLevel 1Chords & Harmony

The C major chord is one of the first open chords every guitarist learns. It uses three fingers and produces a bright, full sound that's foundational to countless songs.

Place your ring finger on the 3rd fret of the A string, middle finger on the 2nd fret of the D string, and index finger on the 1st fret of the B string. Strum from the A string down.

WHERE THIS FITS: C major is the gateway to the key of C — the only major key with no sharps or flats. It connects to G major, A minor, and F major. Together these four chords open up thousands of songs.
