# Foundation Phase — Video Content Needs

These are the moments in Foundation where a video would significantly enhance learning.
Ayla to source/provide these. Placeholders are live on the site.

---

## Block 01: How to Learn

1. **"The Three Barriers" overview** (30-60s)
   - Quick visual montage: someone reading about guitar vs playing (absence of mass), trying a barre chord on day 1 and grimacing (steep gradient), looking confused at sheet music (misunderstood word)
   - Style: warm, relatable, not preachy

## Block 02: Learning a Language

2. **"Just Listen" demonstration** (30-45s)
   - Someone listening to music with eyes closed, reacting naturally
   - Then trying to pick out instruments, feeling the rhythm
   - Style: intimate, close-up, acoustic environment

## Block 03: The Language of Music

3. **"Half Step vs Whole Step" on guitar** (20-30s)
   - Close-up of fretboard showing one fret (half step) vs two frets (whole step)
   - Playing each so you hear the difference
   - Style: clean, well-lit fretboard, good audio

4. **"Finding the Home Note"** (20-30s)
   - Playing a note, then another, resolving back to the root
   - Showing that "home" feeling in real time
   - Style: simple, one guitar, clear audio

## Block 04: The Language of Guitar

5. **"String Names" visual** (15-20s)
   - Each string played open, with the note name appearing on screen
   - "Eddie Ate Dynamite Good Bye Eddie" mnemonic animated
   - Style: bright, fun, maybe slightly playful

6. **"The Octave at Fret 12"** (20-30s)
   - Playing open string, then fret 12 — same note, higher
   - Showing the double dots on the fretboard
   - Style: close-up, clear comparison

## Block 05: The Tool

7. **"Guitar Parts Tour"** (30-45s)
   - Slow pan over a guitar: headstock, tuning pegs, neck, fretboard, frets, body, sound hole, bridge, nut
   - Each part labelled as it's shown
   - Style: well-lit, slow, almost reverent

8. **"How to Sit" demonstration** (30-45s)
   - Someone sitting down, positioning the guitar, showing correct posture
   - Side angle showing the neck angle, arm position
   - Style: relaxed, natural, easy to follow

9. **"The Rest Stroke" close-up** (20-30s)
   - Extreme close-up of thumb pushing through a string and resting on the next
   - The note ringing out clearly
   - Style: macro shot, beautiful audio, maybe slow-motion

---

## Notes for sourcing

- Videos don't need to be professional — authentic and clear beats polished
- 15-60 seconds each is ideal
- Good audio matters more than good video for music content
- If you find something close but not perfect, send it anyway — we can use it and replace later
- YouTube embeds are fine (no need to host ourselves)
