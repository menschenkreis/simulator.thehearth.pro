A Minor Pentatonic Consolidation

Make the scale feel musical, familiar, and useful for jamming. This is about repetition, not rushing into more theory.

Practice commitment 20 minutes a day. Clean, calm, repeatable.
20min/day

The Simple Rule

Tune in, repeat the A minor pentatonic map, make one small musical phrase, then end with jamming or a song idea.

Daily 20-Minute Practice

Tune In · 2 minutes

  • Tune the guitar.
  • Check shoulders, jaw, breath, left hand, and right hand.
  • Play open strings slowly and listen for clean sound.

A Root Notes · 4 minutes

  • Find A on the low E string and A string.
  • Play A, pause, and listen.
  • Make A sound musical with rhythm.

A Minor Pentatonic Map · 5 minutes

  • Practise the current shape slowly.
  • Say the root note when you land on A.
  • If the full shape is messy, use only two strings.

Right-Hand Pattern · 4 minutes

  • Choose one right-hand pattern.
  • Keep the pulse steady and relaxed.
  • Try it over A minor or an A note.

Tiny Jam · 5 minutes

  • Play only A roots first.
  • Add one nearby pentatonic note, then one more.
  • Make a tiny phrase and repeat it.

Extra Drills To Rotate

Use these only when there is energy. Do not force all of them every day.

E and A String Landmarks

  • Practise note locations slowly.
  • Say the note names out loud.

A / E / C / D On Top Strings

  • Find the notes on the top two strings.
  • Make two-note patterns.

Marcello-Style Scale Warm-Up

  • Play slowly.
  • Aim for clean sound, not speed.

Finger-Independence Drill

  • Keep it tiny.
  • Stop before frustration.

Gentle Flamenco Feel

  • Use this for energy and rhythm.
  • Keep it playful.

Call And Response

  • Play a small phrase.
  • Answer it with another small phrase.
