# Co-Learning Teacher Mode V1

## Purpose

The Hearth Mastery is not only for Ayla's private guitar learning.

It is also becoming a live teaching aid for lessons with Jen.

This matters because Ayla is not teaching from a distant expert position. She is learning ahead, preparing carefully, then guiding Jen through the next safe step.

The app should support that honestly.

## Core Insight

Ayla and Jen are learning near each other.

Ayla's role is:

- self-study ahead of the lesson
- prepare the next gradient
- notice gaps during the real session
- guide Jen through musical contact
- log what happened
- let the app help decide the next move

This is not a weakness.

It is a real teaching mode:

`co-learning with responsibility`

The app should help the guide stay one step ahead without pretending she knows everything already.

## Teaching Workflow

### 1. Prepare

Before the lesson, Ayla checks:

- Jen's last lesson notes
- known gaps
- assigned tasks
- current Journey level
- one concept Ayla needs to study ahead
- one practical action Jen can complete

### 2. Teach

During the lesson, the human relationship stays central.

The app can support, but not replace:

- listening
- demonstration
- correction
- encouragement
- body awareness
- musical play

### 3. Reflect

After the lesson, Ayla logs:

- what was reviewed
- what was learned
- what was confusing
- what Jen enjoyed
- what Jen wants to do
- technical gaps
- musical sparks
- private teacher notes

### 4. Prepare The Next Gradient

The app should convert the reflection into:

- next review point
- next tiny physical task
- next concept to clarify
- next creative challenge
- next source/video/book to prepare
- next possible lesson plan

## Student Memory Needed

For each student profile, store:

- current level
- current lesson or path position
- lesson notes
- private teacher notes
- concept ratings
- task ratings
- assigned home tasks
- musical interests
- gaps
- breakthroughs
- questions to revisit

For Ayla-as-teacher, also store:

- what Ayla needs to study before teaching it
- confidence rating for the concept
- source/resource to review
- next explanation draft

## Guide Character Role

The guide character should support both learner and teacher.

Examples:

- "Jen loved call and response. Use that as the doorway again."
- "C chord showed up as a gap. Do not overload her; make it physical."
- "Ayla, prepare the E and A string root-note map before teaching it."
- "The pentatonic shape worked musically, but the explanation needs a simpler version."

The guide should not pretend to be a replacement for Ayla.

It should act like a calm lesson assistant.

## Feature Ideas From The Lesson

### Chord Identifier

A fretboard tool where the learner/teacher selects strings and frets, and the app identifies possible chord names.

It should show:

- chord name
- notes included
- intervals
- root possibilities
- why the name changes depending on context
- related shapes nearby
- source-backed explanation

This belongs between:

- Doing: what the hands are playing
- Study: what the theory means
- Knowing: source-backed explanation

### Tab + Notation Pairing

The app should often show guitar tab beside standard notation.

Purpose:

- help guitarists see how guitar writing and formal music notation relate
- make reading music less abstract
- support Study without making it feel detached from the instrument

This belongs especially in:

- Study
- Knowing
- Foundation threshold
- Doing drill detail pages

## Product Recommendation

Add a future mode or surface called something like:

- Teacher Prep
- Lesson Companion
- Student Path
- Guided Teaching

This does not need to be a separate app node yet.

For now, it can live inside Journey and Hearth:

- Journey tracks the student's path.
- Hearth reflects on what happened.
- Teacher Prep suggests the next safe step.
