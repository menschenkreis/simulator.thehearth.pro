## Core Teaching Principle

Drills must return to music.

A hard exercise by itself can feel discouraging. But when it feeds into a tiny jam, call-and-response, riff, or chord-and-melody exchange, it makes sense.

The app should treat technique drills as:

`training fragments that lead back into musical contact`

Not:

`isolated punishments`

## Ideal Lesson Shape For Jen

1. Tune in: body scan, stretch, tune guitar, open-string sound.
2. Review: last homework or last musical idea.
3. Student doorway: what Jen wants to work on, such as right-hand patterns.
4. Core concept: one clear idea only.
5. Physical drill: small enough to succeed.
6. Music application: riff, chord pattern, call-and-response, or jam.
7. Reflect: what worked, what was hard, what to practise next.
