## The Idea

We are using the guitar like a language.

Today:

- the right hand can hold a rhythm pattern
- root notes are home points
- the E and A strings are highways
- the A minor pentatonic is a five-note vocabulary
- a riff is a small idea that wants to come back

## Right-Hand Patterns

Fingerpicking names:

```text
p = thumb
i = index
m = middle
a = ring
```

Pattern 1:

```text
p i m i
```

Pattern 2:

```text
p i m a
```

Pattern 3:

```text
p i m a m i
```

Strumming names:

```text
D = down
U = up
```

Pattern 1:

```text
D D D D
```

Pattern 2:

```text
D DU D DU
```

Pattern 3:

```text
D D U U D U
```

The right hand gives the music pulse.

## E And A String Notes

Low E string:

```text
0 E | 1 F | 3 G | 5 A | 7 B | 8 C | 10 D | 12 E
```

A string:

```text
0 A | 2 B | 3 C | 5 D | 7 E | 8 F | 10 G | 12 A
```

The 12th fret is the octave: the note becomes itself again.

## A Minor Pentatonic

Pentatonic means five notes.

A minor pentatonic uses:

```text
A C D E G
```

Shape:

```text
e|----------------5-8-|
B|------------5-8-----|
G|--------5-7---------|
D|----5-7-------------|
A|-5-7----------------|
E|-5-8----------------|
```

Home notes are A:

```text
e|----------------5(A)-8-|
B|-------------5-----8---|
G|---------5-----7-------|
D|-----5-----7(A)--------|
A|-5-----7---------------|
E|-5(A)-8----------------|
```

## Riff Recipe

1. Choose three notes.
2. Choose one rhythm or right-hand pattern.
3. Repeat it.
4. Change the ending.

Example:

```text
e|----------------|
B|----------------|
G|------5-7-5-----|
D|--7-7-------7---|
A|----------------|
E|----------------|
```

## Home Practice

1. Keep one right-hand pattern alive.
2. Find notes on the E and A strings.
3. Keep one pentatonic riff alive.
4. Play call and response with yourself:

```text
Question phrase
pause
Answer phrase
pause
```

Remember:

The scale is not the music yet. The music starts when you choose, repeat, pause, and answer.
