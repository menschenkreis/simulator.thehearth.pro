**Supporting/Reference:**

- #21 Berklee Jazz Chord Dictionary — Rick Peckham (100+ movable jazz voicings)
- #29 Ultimate Chord Chart — Phillip Facoline (quick chord lookup)
- #42 Picture Chord Encyclopedia (2,600+ chord voicings with photos)
- #48 Rooksby — How to Write Songs on Guitar (chord progressions, song structure theory)

**Structure:** Topics would be:
- Intervals & Scales (Wolfsohn + Belkadi + Grimoire)

- Keys & Circle of Fifths (Wolfsohn)
- Modes (Nelson + Belkadi)
- Rhythm Theory (Wolfsohn Ch. on time signatures)
- Song Structure (Rooksby)
- Reading Music (Leavitt methods)
