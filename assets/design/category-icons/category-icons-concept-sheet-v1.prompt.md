# Category Icons Concept Sheet v1

Purpose: design reference for the Journey roadmap category icons.

Credit note: this was generated as one contact sheet instead of ten separate final icons to keep exploration cheaper.

Prompt:

Create one polished concept sheet showing 10 circular category icon medallions in a unified style: Rhythm, Chords & Harmony, Scales, Technique, Improvisation, Picking, Fingerstyle, Theory, Reading, Integration.

Use a clean dark warm background and a simple contact sheet grid. Use ten separate circular icons, each with a clear symbol only, no words or letters.

- Rhythm: pulse wave and metronome beat
- Chords & Harmony: interlocking chord shapes or harmonic rings
- Scales: fretboard ladder with root dots
- Technique: relaxed hand and clean spark
- Improvisation: flowing musical phrase swirl
- Picking: guitar pick crossing strings
- Fingerstyle: fingertips plucking strings
- Theory: compass or diagram with musical relationships
- Reading: tab or notation page
- Integration: several small musical elements joining into one circle

Style: refined engraved storybook medallions matching the warm Hearth simulator node-image spirit, cleaner and less ornate, carved brass, wood, gold linework, slightly magical but disciplined, premium Apple-like clarity.

Avoid: text, labels, letters, watermark, black square backgrounds inside icons, excessive frills, busy detail that disappears at small UI sizes.
